## What a Thai Herbal Inhaler Actually Is

A yadom is a traditional Thai herbal inhaler: a small, portable tube packed with aromatic botanicals that you hold below your nostril and breathe in slowly. No smoke, no heat, nothing to plug in. You uncap it, take a few slow breaths, and the aromatics do the rest.

The tradition is centuries old in Thailand, where yadom is as everyday as chewing gum. The Smiling Tiger version is a modern, premium upgrade on that tradition: handcrafted in Koh Samui from organic botanicals and quality essential oils, with seven distinct scent profiles built for different moods and moments. If you want the full background, [our guide to what yadom is](/blog/what-is-yadom-thai-herbal-inhaler) covers the history and the craft.

For a gift, the key detail is this: it is the kind of object that becomes part of a daily ritual. Once someone finds a scent they love, they carry it everywhere.
